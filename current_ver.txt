1.8.1-ford.1
FORD EDITION (first release of this fork)

Google Drive
- Save sync: upload a mod's saves to your Google Drive and get
  them back on another PC (your current saves are backed up first)
- Auto-sync: uploads saves when you close a mod, offers newer
  saves from your other PCs before you play
- Launcher data sync: ratings, statuses, Reddit links, pins,
  icons, backgrounds, covers, play time and settings
- New PC: lists the mods to reimport and gives them back their
  settings once they're added

Mods
- 1-5 star ratings, status labels, a Reddit link per mod
- Sorting and filters, duplicate warning on import
- Custom icon and background for each mod
- Grab images from a mod's Reddit post by pasting them in
- Right-click menu on mods with the common actions
- Storage panel with cleanup of logs, caches and temp files

Look and feel
- Foldable Main and Mods lists, hideable Main links,
  sidebar options
- DDLC-style dialogs, click sound on every mod row
- Fixed the screen staying blurred after "No save found!"
- Updates now come from github.com/fordtoxic/DokiModManager
